Choosing what a notarized session reveals.

The Platform Verifier checks that the revealed ranges and the commitments TILE the transcript: every byte accounted for, no gap and no overlap. A gap is where a prover hides bytes, so a session that leaves one is refused – which means the selection here is not a disclosure preference, it is a correctness requirement. Choose the wrong ranges and no honest ceremony verifies at all.

Every layout below therefore names only what it REVEALS, and the commitments are derived as the complement. Tiling then holds by construction rather than by inspection.

Nothing here is applied on anyone’s behalf. A prover notarizing a ceremony session calls these and hands the result to prover_generic; a prover doing something else states its own. In Rust that prover will be the GitHub Token-Exchange Service, for the token session. The other three sessions are the browser’s.
